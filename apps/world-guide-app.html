World Guide: Offline AI Travel

A private travel guide that writes guides for countries, cities, and landmarks on the device with Apple Intelligence, then keeps them for offline use — with audio walking tours, day-by-day plans, Lens, and widgets. No account, no tracking.

Overview

World Guide 2.0 rebuilds the app around on-device intelligence. Guides are generated on the iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence and saved for offline reading, so the app keeps working in airplane mode and without roaming data.

Coverage spans 244 countries, 800+ cities, and thousands of landmarks, in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, and Japanese. World Guide Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, and is shared through Family Sharing.

Core experience

  • On-device guides for countries, cities, and landmarks
  • Audio walking tours on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Day-by-day plans grouped into walkable routes
  • Lens: point the camera at a landmark to hear its story
  • Siri, City of the Day, and Travel Passport widgets
  • Phrasebooks and offline Apple Maps imagery

Privacy and support

Guides are written and stored on the device, and the app requires no account. Dedicated pages cover Apple Intelligence requirements, camera and location permissions, purchases and restore, offline content, and data requests.
