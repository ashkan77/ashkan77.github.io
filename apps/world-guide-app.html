Overview

World Guide App is built as a structured destination guide. The app organizes countries, cities, attractions, maps, weather, and useful travel information into a mobile experience that is easy to browse before or during a trip.

The product direction emphasizes clarity: users should quickly understand where they are in the content structure, move between destinations, and read attraction information without feeling overwhelmed.

Core experience

  • Country, city, and attraction browsing
  • Organized destination information
  • Maps and weather updates
  • Favorite cities and attractions
  • Travel-focused mobile interface

Privacy and support

Dedicated pages explain optional location, online map and weather services, locally stored favorites, contact information, deletion requests, troubleshooting, and content-accuracy guidance.
