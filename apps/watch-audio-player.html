Watch Audio Player

Your audio, on your wrist. Import audio on iPhone, send it to Apple Watch, and listen offline with Apple’s native Now Playing controls and Digital Crown volume — no account, no ads, no tracking.

Overview

Watch Audio Player moves audio you already own from iPhone to Apple Watch so you can listen away from your phone. Transfers are verified for file integrity before they are marked complete, and the Watch library can be managed directly on the wrist.

Version 2.1 refines the Watch experience with native watchOS controls, Digital Crown volume, improved layouts across screen sizes, and more reliable transfers.

Core experience

  • Import AAC, AIFF, CAF, FLAC, M4A, M4B, MP3, and WAV
  • Send one item or a whole queue to Apple Watch
  • Integrity-checked transfers
  • Browse, search, favorite, rename, and remove on the Watch
  • Transfer and library widgets on iPhone and Apple Watch

Privacy and support

Personal audio remains under the user's control and the developer does not collect app data. Dedicated pages provide file-transfer guidance, troubleshooting, purchase information, and the complete privacy policy.
