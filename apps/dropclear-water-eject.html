DropClear: Water Eject

A focused iPhone utility that plays controlled low-frequency sound and uses haptic feedback where available to help move trapped water away from the speaker area after splashes or light moisture exposure.

Overview

DropClear is designed for the moment an iPhone speaker sounds muffled after rain, a splash, or other light moisture exposure. The app keeps the workflow direct: prepare the device, start a controlled clearing session, and listen again when the session finishes.

The app is a speaker utility, not a repair or waterproofing service. It cannot diagnose internal damage, restore damaged hardware, or guarantee that liquid has been removed from the device.

Core experience

  • Focused water-ejection sound sessions
  • Low-frequency audio designed for speaker movement
  • Haptic feedback where the device and system allow it
  • Clear session state and simple controls
  • Guidance for safer device positioning and repeat attempts

Responsible use

Use DropClear only when the device can safely play audio. Disconnect charging cables and wired accessories, keep the speaker area unobstructed, and stop immediately if the device becomes unusually warm, produces abnormal sound, or appears physically damaged.

For significant liquid exposure, charging warnings, persistent audio problems, or suspected hardware damage, follow Apple guidance and contact an authorized service provider.

Technologies

  • SwiftUI
  • AVFoundation audio-session and playback workflows
  • Programmatic tone generation
  • Core Haptics and system haptic feedback
  • State-driven session controls
  • App Store product presentation

Privacy and support

DropClear does not require an account or personal profile. Dedicated pages explain local processing, Apple diagnostics, safe-use guidance, audio-routing troubleshooting, and what to include in a support request.
