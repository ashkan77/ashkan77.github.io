Overview

BMI Checker Plus is designed to make body mass index checks simple and understandable while supporting history, hydration, reminders, widgets, exports, and optional Apple Health workflows.

The interface presents calculations clearly and avoids medical claims so the experience remains useful, responsible, and easy to understand.

Core experience

  • BMI calculation and readable categories
  • History and trend presentation
  • Hydration tracking and reminders
  • Apple Health and Apple Watch workflows
  • Widgets and PDF or CSV export

Privacy and support

The developer does not collect BMI, hydration, history, or Health data from the app. Dedicated pages explain HealthKit permissions, private Apple services, deletion controls, troubleshooting, and the non-diagnostic safety position.
