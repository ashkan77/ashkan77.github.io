Overview

Converter One is built for fast everyday conversions. The product direction is simple: reduce the steps between a value, a unit, and the result. The interface gives priority to clarity, predictable input, and readable output.

It covers length, area, volume, weight, temperature, speed, time, pressure, energy, power, force, angle, data, data rate, frequency, fuel, density, torque, and more. Version 1.02 improves numeric precision, handling of invalid and extreme values, conversion history, and accessibility.

Core experience

  • 19+ unit categories with instant results
  • Favorites and smart search
  • Conversion history
  • Adaptive layouts and haptic feedback
  • 3D presentation and gestures on Vision Pro
  • Offline, with no data collected

Privacy and support

Conversion values are processed on the device and the developer does not collect app data. Dedicated pages provide troubleshooting, formatting guidance, and the complete privacy policy.
