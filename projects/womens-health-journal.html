Women's Health Journal

A calm, private place to organize health observations, menstrual-cycle history, symptoms, medications, appointments, and everyday notes. The iPhone-first experience is designed around clear records rather than forecasts or AI-generated medical advice.

Product direction

Women's Health Journal focuses on keeping personal health information organized in an accessible, Apple-native interface. Its design favors deliberate logging, observation history, meaningful record relationships, and clear exports for the user's own review.

The initial product is a focused iPhone app with a single paid product offering. An Apple Watch companion and automated AI features are outside the planned first release.

First-release scope

  • Cycle observations and symptom-history journaling, without fertility or period predictions
  • Medication, appointment, journal, and personal health-record organization
  • Reports, record relationships, date-range review, and controlled exports
  • Optional, category-specific Apple Health access when the user grants permission
  • App Lock and privacy-aware access to sensitive information
  • Accessibility-oriented typography, readable forms, and consistent navigation

Privacy-first architecture

The intended experience is offline-first, with health journaling and records handled locally. Any Apple Health access should be individually permissioned and optional; users should not need to enable Health access simply to use the journal.

Apple iCloud integration and the precise categories of data it may synchronize are subject to verification against the final release build. The public privacy disclosures will be finalized only after the implemented data flows and App Store privacy requirements are reviewed.

Engineering focus

  • Swift and SwiftUI with an Apple-native interface
  • iOS 26+ and iPhone-first navigation
  • HealthKit, with consent scoped to the relevant data types
  • Local record storage and protected app access
  • History, reporting, export, and accessibility
  • No AI-driven diagnosis, treatment, or prediction features in version 1.0

Release and safety

This page describes the product scope, not an App Store release certification. Real-device permission tests, privacy and security verification, medical-content review, and signed release checks must be completed before public distribution.

Women's Health Journal is intended for personal recordkeeping. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace professional medical advice.
