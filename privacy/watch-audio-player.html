Watch Audio Player Privacy Policy

Watch Audio Player is designed for private playback of personal audio on Apple Watch. The developer does not collect data from the app.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Data collection

Watch Audio Player does not require an account and does not collect personal data, listening history, file names, audio content, contacts, location, or usage data for the developer.

Personal audio

Audio files you select or transfer are used only to provide file management and playback features on your devices. The developer does not receive or remotely store your personal audio.

Local storage

Imported audio, playback state, library information, and app preferences may be stored locally on the iPhone or Apple Watch. You can remove audio through available app controls or by deleting the app.

Network access

The core playback experience is designed to work offline. Device-to-device transfer and Apple platform services may use system connectivity, but the app is not designed to upload personal audio to a developer-operated server.

Purchases

The App Store processes payment, purchase history, refunds, and Family Sharing. The developer does not receive payment card details.

Diagnostics

Apple or the operating system may provide crash reports, performance diagnostics, and App Store analytics according to the user's Apple settings. The app does not include a third-party advertising or tracking SDK.

Support

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to provide are used only to respond to the request. Do not attach personal audio or other sensitive content unless it is necessary.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Audio content remains under the control of the device owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated if app features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, open Watch Audio Player Support.
