Summary

Watch Audio Player has no account, developer-operated server, advertising, or analytics SDK. It does not collect your audio, file names, listening history, contacts, or location for the developer. This matches its App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Importing audio

You choose audio files from the Files app or the Share Sheet. Supported formats include AAC, AIFF, CAF, FLAC, M4A, M4B, MP3, and WAV. The app keeps a copy in its own storage on your iPhone so it can prepare transfers; the original file stays where it was.

Transfer to Apple Watch

Single files and transfer queues are sent from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch over Apple's built-in iPhone-to-Watch connection. Each file passes an integrity check before the transfer is marked complete. Audio is never sent to a developer-operated server.

Library and playback

Library details such as titles, custom names, and favorites are stored on the device. During playback, watchOS shows the current title in Now Playing, and audio plays through the Apple Watch speaker or connected Bluetooth headphones.

Widgets

iPhone and Apple Watch widgets show transfer and library status using information stored locally on the device.

Purchases

Watch Audio Player is a paid app with no subscriptions. Apple processes the purchase, refunds, and Family Sharing; the developer never receives your payment card details.

Diagnostics and analytics

Watch Audio Player contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not send usage data to a developer-operated server. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Apple's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer crash reports and performance information. Apple also provides aggregated App Store metrics such as downloads and sales. Apple supplies this information in aggregated or de-identified form, and it is used only to fix problems and improve reliability.

Support communication

If you email support, your email address and whatever you choose to include are used only to answer your request. Messages are handled through the developer's Apple iCloud Mail account and kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and keep a reasonable record of it. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and any personal information that is not needed to understand the problem.

Retention and deletion

Audio and library details stay on your devices until you remove them in the app or delete the app from iPhone and Apple Watch, which removes its stored copies. Your original files in Files are not affected. The developer holds no copy of your audio. Support emails are deleted on request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and US state privacy laws may give you the right to know what personal data is processed, to access or receive a copy of it, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Watch Audio Player does not send your app data to the developer, so most of it stays on your devices under your direct control. For information you have sent by email, write to azanjani@me.com from the same address so the request can be verified. Requests are answered within 30 days. Personal information is never sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

Watch Audio Player keeps its data in the app's own container, protected by Apple's platform security, including device encryption and Data Protection tied to your device passcode. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. No storage method is perfectly secure, so only keep information you are comfortable storing on your device.

Children

Watch Audio Player does not require registration and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Audio content remains under the control of the device owner.

Changes to this policy

This policy is reviewed whenever Watch Audio Player adds a feature that affects personal data, Apple platform behavior changes, or the App Store privacy disclosure changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes are published here before the app version that introduces them.

Contact

The developer and data controller for Watch Audio Player is Ashkan Zanjani. For privacy questions or requests, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, visit Watch Audio Player Support. Apple's own processing is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
