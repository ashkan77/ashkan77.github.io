Summary

Converter One has no account, developer-operated server, advertising, or analytics SDK. It does not collect personal information, conversion values, search history, favorites, location, or contacts for the developer. This matches its App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Conversions on your device

Values and units you enter are calculated on the device. The developer never receives them.

Favorites and preferences

Favorites, recent selections, formatting choices, and settings are stored locally so the app remembers your preferences.

Offline operation

Core conversions work without an internet connection, and the app does not upload conversion history or personal information.

Apple Vision Pro and Mac

On Apple Vision Pro, eye and hand input is processed by visionOS. The app receives only standard interaction events, such as a tap, and has no access to camera images or where you look. On a Mac, Converter One uses the same local storage and offline behavior as on iPhone and iPad.

Purchases

Converter One is a paid app. Apple processes the purchase, refunds, and Family Sharing; the developer never receives your payment card details.

Diagnostics and analytics

Converter One contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not send usage data to a developer-operated server. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Apple's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer crash reports and performance information. Apple also provides aggregated App Store metrics such as downloads and sales. Apple supplies this information in aggregated or de-identified form, and it is used only to fix problems and improve reliability.

Support communication

If you email support, your email address and whatever you choose to include are used only to answer your request. Messages are handled through the developer's Apple iCloud Mail account and kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and keep a reasonable record of it. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and any personal information that is not needed to understand the problem.

Retention and deletion

Local favorites and settings stay on your device until you reset them or delete the app. The developer holds no copy of your app data. Support emails are deleted on request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and US state privacy laws may give you the right to know what personal data is processed, to access or receive a copy of it, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Converter One does not send your app data to the developer, so most of it stays on your devices under your direct control. For information you have sent by email, write to azanjani@me.com from the same address so the request can be verified. Requests are answered within 30 days. Personal information is never sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

Converter One keeps its data in the app's own container, protected by Apple's platform security, including device encryption and Data Protection tied to your device passcode. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. No storage method is perfectly secure, so only keep information you are comfortable storing on your device.

Children

Converter One does not require registration and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy is reviewed whenever Converter One adds a feature that affects personal data, Apple platform behavior changes, or the App Store privacy disclosure changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes are published here before the app version that introduces them.

Contact

The developer and data controller for Converter One is Ashkan Zanjani. For privacy questions or requests, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, visit Converter One Support. Apple's own processing is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
