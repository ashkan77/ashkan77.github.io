Converter One Privacy Policy

Converter One is an offline conversion utility. The developer does not collect data from the app.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Data collection

Converter One does not require an account and does not collect personal information, conversion values, search history, favorites, device location, contacts, or usage data for the developer.

Conversion data

Values and units entered into the app are processed on the device to calculate and display results. The developer does not receive the values you enter.

Local preferences

Favorites, recent selections, formatting choices, and app settings may be stored locally so the app can remember your preferences. You can reset app data through available controls or by deleting the app.

Offline operation

The core conversion features are designed to work without an internet connection. The app is not designed to upload conversion history or personal information to a developer-operated server.

Diagnostics

Apple or the operating system may provide crash reports, performance diagnostics, and App Store analytics according to the user's Apple settings. Converter One does not include a third-party advertising or tracking SDK.

Support communication

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to provide are used only to respond to the request. Do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive content.

Children

Converter One is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children and does not require user registration.

Changes

This policy may be updated if features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, open Converter One Support.
