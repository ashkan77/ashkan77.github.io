Summary

BMI Checker Plus has no account, developer-operated server, advertising, or analytics SDK. It does not collect height, weight, BMI results, hydration entries, reminders, or history for the developer. This matches its App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Information you enter

Height, weight, and related details you enter, such as goals and hydration entries, are used to calculate BMI, show your history and charts, and provide general health tips. This information is stored on your device.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health on iPhone or Apple Watch, BMI Checker Plus asks for access through Apple's permission screen, and you choose which categories it can read or write. Health data is used only to provide the features you request. It is never used for advertising or data mining, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can change access at any time in the Health app.

iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is on, your BMI history and app records are stored in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account so they are available on your other devices. Apple stores this data under its own terms, and the developer cannot access your private iCloud data.

Reminders, notifications, and widgets

Reminder schedules, hydration prompts, and widget content are prepared on the device and delivered by iOS and watchOS features. You can change notification permission in Settings at any time.

Exports

PDF and CSV exports are created on the device only when you ask and are shared through the system share sheet to a destination you choose. An exported file is outside the app's protection, so share it carefully.

Medical notice

BMI Checker Plus is a general wellness utility, not a medical device. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency advice. Standard adult BMI categories do not apply to children, teens, or pregnancy.

Diagnostics and analytics

BMI Checker Plus contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not send usage data to a developer-operated server. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Apple's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer crash reports and performance information. Apple also provides aggregated App Store metrics such as downloads and sales. Apple supplies this information in aggregated or de-identified form, and it is used only to fix problems and improve reliability.

Support communication

If you email support, your email address and whatever you choose to include are used only to answer your request. Messages are handled through the developer's Apple iCloud Mail account and kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and keep a reasonable record of it. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and any personal information that is not needed to understand the problem.

Retention and deletion

Records stay on your devices until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Copies in iCloud can be removed through your iCloud storage settings (Settings > [your name] > iCloud). Data written to Apple Health stays in the Health app until you delete it there. The developer holds no copy of your data. Support emails are deleted on request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and US state privacy laws may give you the right to know what personal data is processed, to access or receive a copy of it, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

BMI Checker Plus does not send your app data to the developer, so most of it stays on your devices under your direct control. For information you have sent by email, write to azanjani@me.com from the same address so the request can be verified. Requests are answered within 30 days. Personal information is never sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

BMI Checker Plus keeps its data in the app's own container, protected by Apple's platform security, including device encryption and Data Protection tied to your device passcode. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. No storage method is perfectly secure, so only keep information you are comfortable storing on your device.

Children

BMI Checker Plus is rated 13+ on the App Store and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Adult BMI categories are not designed for young people, so a parent or guardian should supervise any use involving a child or teen.

Changes to this policy

This policy is reviewed whenever BMI Checker Plus adds a feature that affects personal data, Apple platform behavior changes, or the App Store privacy disclosure changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes are published here before the app version that introduces them.

Contact

The developer and data controller for BMI Checker Plus is Ashkan Zanjani. For privacy questions or requests, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, visit BMI Checker Plus Support. Apple's own processing is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
