BMI Checker Plus Privacy Policy

BMI Checker Plus processes health utility information for user-facing features. The developer does not collect data from the app.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Data collection

BMI Checker Plus does not require an account and does not collect height, weight, BMI results, hydration entries, reminders, health history, or other personal data for the developer.

Calculations and history

Values you enter are processed to calculate BMI and related user-facing results. History, preferences, reminders, and other app records may be stored on the device so the app can provide tracking, charts, widgets, and export features.

HealthKit and Apple Health

If you enable Apple Health integration, BMI Checker Plus requests access through Apple's permission interface. You choose which Health categories are shared. Health data is used only for app features you request and is not used for advertising or sold to data brokers.

iCloud and Apple services

If an app version offers iCloud synchronization, data is stored in the user's private Apple account through Apple-provided services. The developer does not receive direct access to the user's private iCloud or Health data. Availability and behavior depend on the installed app version and Apple settings.

Notifications and widgets

Reminder schedules, hydration prompts, and widget data are processed through system features to provide the experience you configure. Notification permission can be changed in system settings.

Diagnostics

Apple or the operating system may provide crash reports, performance diagnostics, and App Store analytics according to the user's Apple settings. The app does not include a third-party advertising or tracking SDK.

Deletion and control

Use available in-app controls to delete history or records, manage Health data in the Apple Health app, change Health and notification permissions in system settings, manage iCloud data through Apple settings, or delete the app.

Medical notice

BMI Checker Plus is a general health utility and is not a medical device. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical advice.

Support communication

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to provide are used only to respond. Avoid sending private health records or sensitive medical information.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should supervise any use involving health information.

Changes

This policy may be updated if app features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, open BMI Checker Plus Support.
