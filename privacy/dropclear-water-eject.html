DropClear: Water Eject Privacy Policy

DropClear is designed as a local speaker utility. It does not require an account, personal profile, or developer-operated cloud service to provide its core water-ejection sound sessions.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Data collection

DropClear is not designed to collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, files, microphone recordings, listening history, advertising identifiers, or water-ejection session content for the developer.

Audio generation

The app generates and plays sound locally on the device to provide the requested speaker-clearing session. DropClear does not need to record microphone input or upload audio to a developer-operated server.

Haptic feedback

Where supported, the app may use Apple system haptic frameworks to provide physical feedback during a session. Haptic events are generated locally and are not transmitted to the developer.

Local settings

App preferences, onboarding state, selected mode, session duration, or similar settings may be stored locally so the app can remember your choices. You can reset local app data through available controls or by deleting the app.

Permissions

The core DropClear experience is not designed to require access to contacts, location, photos, camera, files, or microphone recordings. If a future feature introduces a new permission, the app, App Store disclosure, and this policy must be updated before that behavior is released.

Diagnostics and App Store analytics

Apple or the operating system may provide crash reports, performance diagnostics, and App Store analytics according to the user's Apple settings. This information may be used to investigate defects and improve reliability. DropClear is not designed to include third-party advertising tracking.

Purchases

If the app offers a paid download or in-app purchase, payment processing, receipts, refunds, purchase history, and restoration are handled through Apple's App Store systems. The developer does not receive payment card details.

Support communication

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to understand and respond to the request. Remove serial numbers, payment details, passwords, and other unnecessary sensitive information from messages and screenshots.

Deletion and control

You can remove locally stored DropClear data by using available reset controls or deleting the app. For deletion requests concerning support emails, contact support from the same email address used for the original communication.

Children

DropClear does not require registration and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact support with privacy concerns.

Changes

This policy may be updated if DropClear features, Apple platform behavior, data practices, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting and safe-use guidance, open DropClear Support.
