Summary

DropClear works entirely on your iPhone. It does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, files, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, or session history for the developer, and it has no developer-operated server. This matches its App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Sound sessions and speaker test

The app generates and plays sound locally to run water-ejection sessions and the before-and-after speaker test. Nothing is recorded: DropClear does not use the microphone and does not upload audio.

Haptic feedback

Where supported, DropClear uses Apple's haptic frameworks to give physical feedback during a session. Haptic events are generated on the device and are not transmitted to the developer.

Settings stored on your device

Session length, onboarding state, preferences, and your DropClear Pro status are stored locally so the app remembers your choices. Purchase status is confirmed on the device through Apple's StoreKit.

Permissions

DropClear does not ask for access to contacts, location, photos, the camera, files, or the microphone. If a future version needs a new permission, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.

Purchases

DropClear is free to download. DropClear Pro is an optional in-app purchase processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment card details; Apple handles receipts, refunds, and restoring purchases.

Diagnostics and analytics

DropClear: Water Eject contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not send usage data to a developer-operated server. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Apple's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer crash reports and performance information. Apple also provides aggregated App Store metrics such as downloads and sales. Apple supplies this information in aggregated or de-identified form, and it is used only to fix problems and improve reliability.

Support communication

If you email support, your email address and whatever you choose to include are used only to answer your request. Messages are handled through the developer's Apple iCloud Mail account and kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and keep a reasonable record of it. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and any personal information that is not needed to understand the problem.

Retention and deletion

Local settings stay on your iPhone until you reset them or delete the app. The developer holds no copy of your app data. Support emails are deleted on request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and US state privacy laws may give you the right to know what personal data is processed, to access or receive a copy of it, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

DropClear: Water Eject does not send your app data to the developer, so most of it stays on your devices under your direct control. For information you have sent by email, write to azanjani@me.com from the same address so the request can be verified. Requests are answered within 30 days. Personal information is never sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

DropClear: Water Eject keeps its data in the app's own container, protected by Apple's platform security, including device encryption and Data Protection tied to your device passcode. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. No storage method is perfectly secure, so only keep information you are comfortable storing on your device.

Children

DropClear does not require registration and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. A parent or guardian may contact the developer with any privacy question.

Changes to this policy

This policy is reviewed whenever DropClear: Water Eject adds a feature that affects personal data, Apple platform behavior changes, or the App Store privacy disclosure changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes are published here before the app version that introduces them.

Contact

The developer and data controller for DropClear: Water Eject is Ashkan Zanjani. For privacy questions or requests, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, visit DropClear: Water Eject Support. Apple's own processing is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
