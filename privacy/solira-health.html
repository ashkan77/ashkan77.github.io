Solira Health Privacy Policy

Solira Health is designed as a privacy-first, non-diagnostic health companion. This policy describes the current product direction and will be reviewed before any public release.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Summary

Solira Health is designed to keep health logs, hydration, mood, symptoms, medication records, journal entries, goals, habits, body metrics, sleep, activity, and related insights under the user's control. The product is not designed to sell health information or use it for third-party advertising.

HealthKit and Apple Health

If Apple Health integration is enabled, Solira Health requests access through Apple's permission interface. You choose which Health categories are shared and can change access in system settings or the Health app.

Local storage

Health entries, preferences, reminders, summaries, and app state may be stored locally to provide the features you choose. CloudKit synchronization is not part of the current release plan. Any future sync feature would require this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures to be updated before release.

Apple intelligence features

Solira Health may use Apple-provided intelligence capabilities for user-requested summaries, explanations, questions, and review-first logging flows. The product is designed to avoid automatic saving of generated content without user review and confirmation.

Permissions

The app may request Health, notifications, microphone, speech recognition, camera, photos, files, or other system permissions only when a related feature needs them. You can deny or change permissions in system settings.

Diagnostics

Apple or the operating system may provide crash reports, performance diagnostics, and App Store analytics according to the user's Apple settings. The product is not designed to include third-party advertising tracking.

Deletion and control

Use available in-app controls to delete records, revoke permissions in system settings, manage Health data in the Health app, or delete the app. For support-message deletion requests, contact support from the same email address used for the communication.

Health and safety

Solira Health is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical advice. For medical concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.

Support communication

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to provide are used only to respond. Avoid sending private medical records, passwords, payment information, or unnecessary sensitive health details.

Children

Solira Health is not directed to children and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact support with concerns.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features, data practices, or platform requirements change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email azanjani@me.com. For product guidance, open Solira Health Support.
