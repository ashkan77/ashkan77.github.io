Solira Health Privacy Policy

Solira Health is a privacy-first, non-diagnostic health companion. Health information stays on your iPhone, is never sold, and is never used for advertising.

In development. Solira Health is not yet available on the App Store. This policy describes the current product direction and will be reviewed again before any public release.

Summary

Solira Health is designed to keep health logs, hydration, mood, symptoms, medication records, journal entries, goals, habits, body metrics, sleep, activity, and related insights under your control. The app has no developer-operated server, advertising, or analytics SDK, and it never sells health information or uses it for advertising.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Solira Health requests access through Apple's permission screen, and you choose which categories are shared. Health data is used only for the features you request. It is never used for advertising or data mining, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can change access in the Health app or Settings.

Local storage

Health entries, preferences, reminders, summaries, and app state are stored locally on the device. Sensitive health data is not synchronized with CloudKit or iCloud. If a future version adds sync, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before release.

Ask Solira and Apple intelligence

Ask Solira uses Apple's on-device intelligence for summaries, explanations, questions, and review-first logging. It does not send health information to third-party AI providers. Generated content is never saved or written to Apple Health without your review and confirmation.

Permissions

The app may request Health, notifications, microphone, speech recognition, camera, photos, files, or other system permissions only when a related feature needs them. You can deny or change permissions in Settings.

Health and safety

Solira Health is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical advice. For medical concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.

Diagnostics and analytics

Solira Health contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not send usage data to a developer-operated server. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Apple's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer crash reports and performance information. Apple also provides aggregated App Store metrics such as downloads and sales. Apple supplies this information in aggregated or de-identified form, and it is used only to fix problems and improve reliability.

Support communication

If you email support, your email address and whatever you choose to include are used only to answer your request. Messages are handled through the developer's Apple iCloud Mail account and kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and keep a reasonable record of it. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and any personal information that is not needed to understand the problem.

Retention and deletion

Records stay on your iPhone until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Data written to Apple Health stays in the Health app until you delete it there, and you can revoke permissions in Settings at any time. The developer holds no copy of your health data. Support emails are deleted on request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and US state privacy laws may give you the right to know what personal data is processed, to access or receive a copy of it, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Solira Health does not send your app data to the developer, so most of it stays on your devices under your direct control. For information you have sent by email, write to azanjani@me.com from the same address so the request can be verified. Requests are answered within 30 days. Personal information is never sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

Solira Health keeps its data in the app's own container, protected by Apple's platform security, including device encryption and Data Protection tied to your device passcode. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. No storage method is perfectly secure, so only keep information you are comfortable storing on your device.

Children

Solira Health is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. A parent or guardian may contact the developer with concerns.

Changes to this policy

This policy is reviewed whenever Solira Health adds a feature that affects personal data, Apple platform behavior changes, or the App Store privacy disclosure changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes are published here before the app version that introduces them.

Contact

The developer and data controller for Solira Health is Ashkan Zanjani. For privacy questions or requests, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, visit Solira Health Support. Apple's own processing is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
