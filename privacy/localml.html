Summary

LocalML is provided by Ashkan Zanjani. Core workspace data stays on your device unless you explicitly export or share it or enable optional iCloud chat sync. LocalML contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs, uses no third-party AI model provider, and the developer operates no server that receives your content.

On-device model processing

LocalML generates responses on your device. When Apple Intelligence is available, it uses Apple's Foundation Models framework. If your version of LocalML lets you import a compatible model file, that model also runs entirely on your device.

Prompts, documents, chat history, attachments, and voice transcripts are not sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Hugging Face, or any other third-party AI provider. If no on-device model is ready, LocalML explains the device, language, region, operating-system, or model requirement instead of sending the request elsewhere.

Imported model files

If your version supports importing a model, you choose the file yourself through the Files app. LocalML copies it into its own storage on the device and uses it only for local text generation. Imported model files, and the prompts and responses produced with them, are not uploaded, and model files are not included in iCloud chat sync. You can remove imported models in the app or by deleting the app.

Information stored locally

Depending on the features you use, chats, prompts, projects, imported-document text, local search chunks, OCR text, citations, generated artifacts and images, optional memory, settings, local diagnostics, and temporary voice files may be stored on your device. This information stays local unless you explicitly share or export it or enable the optional iCloud chat sync described below.

You can review and delete local categories in Settings > Data & Privacy. Exports and feedback reports use Apple's system share sheet; LocalML does not choose a destination or upload them automatically.

Optional iCloud chat sync

iCloud chat sync is off by default. If you enable it, LocalML stores supported conversation fields in the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account so conversations can continue across your Apple devices. The developer does not operate a separate sync server and cannot access users' private CloudKit records.

Synced chat payloads can include conversation and message identifiers, titles, previews, message text, sent voice transcripts, saved requests and responses, timestamps, message status, routing and display metadata, and structured answers or artifacts stored in a chat. Attachment files, raw imported documents, raw OCR sources, source-citation records, generated-image files, projects, the document index, memory, settings, local diagnostics, composer drafts, imported model files, and raw voice audio do not sync as separate fields.

Schema version 3 stores inline chat payloads and sensitive preview and deletion fields with CloudKit encrypted values. Larger encoded chat payloads use assets in the private CloudKit database. Integrity and ordering metadata such as schema version, revision, checksum, and update time remains ordinary record metadata and contains no chat text.

Web Source Access

Web Source Access is off by default. If you enable it, LocalML can contact a public HTTPS address you provide, or a page selected through General Web Research, to retrieve readable source text. The website receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address and applies its own logging, terms, retention, and privacy policy. LocalML uses an ephemeral connection without stored cookies, credentials, cache, or a referrer and does not copy fetched pages to a developer-operated server.

General Web Research and Brave Search

General Web Research is off by default and requires Web Source Access plus a Brave Search API key that you save in the device Keychain. When you request public-web research, an on-device Apple model may create one bounded query. LocalML sends Brave Search the query, basic language or region parameters, and your API credential. It does not send Brave your chat history, attachments, documents, memory, fetched page text, voice transcript, model answer, or precise location.

Brave can associate the request with your Brave Search API account and may retain or process the query under its own terms and privacy policy. For App Store privacy reporting, the bounded query is treated as linked Search History used only for App Functionality and never for tracking. LocalML contacts a limited number of selected websites separately and writes the final answer on device.

Apple Online Research and Apple Maps

Apple Online Research is on by default and can be turned off in Settings > Data & Privacy. For recognized place, address, landmark, or point-of-interest requests, LocalML sends a bounded version of the request to Apple Maps, without your chat history, documents, memory, voice transcript, or precise device location. Apple Maps results are used as sources for the on-device answer, and Apple handles them under its own terms and privacy practices.

Optional Spotlight indexing

Spotlight integration is off by default. If enabled, LocalML indexes only approved document and generated-image metadata. It never indexes raw chat transcripts, raw document contents, screenshot OCR, voice transcripts, memory, or internal prompts. The named Spotlight index uses Apple Data Protection.

Camera, photos, microphone, and speech

Camera access is requested only when you choose to capture an image for visual analysis or OCR. The system photo picker is used when you choose an existing image. Selected images are analyzed on device with Apple Vision; LocalML does not use faces for identity or emotion inference and does not automatically open barcode content.

Microphone and Speech access are requested only when you start dictation or a voice conversation. Transcription uses an on-device Apple Speech path with no third-party fallback. Spoken replies use installed Apple system voices. Temporary voice files are protected on device and included in LocalML cleanup controls.

Feedback reports

LocalML can prepare a response-feedback report, but nothing is uploaded automatically. If you explicitly include an Apple Foundation Models diagnostic, the protected report may contain the in-memory session transcript and is shared only after you choose a destination in the system share sheet.

Purchases

LocalML shows no advertising. If LocalML offers optional paid upgrades, such as a one-time Pro unlock, Apple processes the purchase through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment card details, and purchase status is confirmed on the device through Apple's StoreKit.

Diagnostics and analytics

LocalML contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not send usage data to a developer-operated server. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Apple's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer crash reports and performance information. Apple also provides aggregated App Store metrics such as downloads and sales. Apple supplies this information in aggregated or de-identified form, and it is used only to fix problems and improve reliability.

Support communication

If you email support, your email address and whatever you choose to include are used only to answer your request. Messages are handled through the developer's Apple iCloud Mail account and kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and keep a reasonable record of it. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and any personal information that is not needed to understand the problem.

Retention and deletion

Local information is kept until you delete it, remove the app, or iOS removes the app's data. LocalML provides category-specific and full deletion controls. Turning iCloud chat sync off stops future sync but does not automatically delete existing iCloud records. While sync is on, LocalML records deletion requests locally and retries temporary CloudKit failures until Apple confirms the deletion. A CloudKit deletion record becomes eligible for cleanup after 365 days when no live conversation record remains, and may remain longer until a later sync performs the cleanup.

Apple may retain iCloud, system diagnostic, Siri, Speech, Spotlight, Image Playground, or Apple Maps information under Apple's own terms, settings, and retention practices. Support emails are deleted on request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and US state privacy laws may give you the right to know what personal data is processed, to access or receive a copy of it, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

LocalML does not send your app data to the developer, so most of it stays on your devices under your direct control. If you use General Web Research, Brave receives your bounded search query under its own privacy policy, so requests about that data should be sent to Brave. For information you have sent by email, write to azanjani@me.com from the same address so the request can be verified. Requests are answered within 30 days. Personal information is never sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

LocalML keeps its data in the app's own container, protected by Apple's platform security, including device encryption and Data Protection tied to your device passcode. Sensitive iCloud sync fields use CloudKit encrypted values, and API keys you save are stored in the Keychain. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. No storage method is perfectly secure, so only keep information you are comfortable storing on your device.

Children

LocalML is a general-purpose productivity app and is not designed or marketed for children or the Kids Category. Do not provide information about a child unless you have the authority and legal basis to do so.

Changes to this policy

This policy is reviewed whenever LocalML adds a feature that affects personal data, Apple platform behavior changes, or the App Store privacy disclosure changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes are published here before the app version that introduces them.

Contact
