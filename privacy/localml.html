LocalML Privacy Policy

LocalML is designed as a privacy-first Apple-native workspace. This policy describes the planned data practices for LocalML 1.0 and will be reviewed before release whenever product behavior changes.

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Summary

LocalML is designed to keep your AI workspace under your control. Core workspace data stays on your device unless you explicitly export or share it. Optional iCloud chat sync can copy the supported parts of conversations to the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account. LocalML does not use advertising SDKs, third-party advertising tracking, or third-party AI model providers.

Content you choose to use

The app may process text you enter and files, documents, photos, screenshots, camera captures, or audio you explicitly select. This content is used only to perform the requested feature, such as answering a question, summarizing a document, reviewing an image, transcribing speech, creating an artifact, or exporting content.

Apple intelligence processing

LocalML is designed around Apple-provided intelligence and system frameworks. Processing is performed on device whenever the selected feature and device support it. If a user explicitly invokes an Apple-provided feature that requires Apple services, the selected content may be handled by Apple under Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

LocalML is not designed to send user content to third-party AI model providers.

Local storage

Chats, projects, project preferences, scoped memory, saved answers, imported-document data, generated artifacts, diagnostics, and app settings may be stored locally so LocalML can provide, recover, and delete its features. When iCloud chat sync is off, new chat changes remain on this device unless you explicitly export or share them. Turning sync off does not delete chat records already stored in iCloud.

Optional iCloud chat sync

iCloud chat sync is off by default. If you enable it in Settings > Data & Privacy and iCloud is available, LocalML uses the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account so supported conversation data can continue across your Apple devices. The developer does not operate a separate server for this sync.

What syncs: conversation and message identifiers; conversation titles and previews; message text, including sent voice transcripts and text pasted or quoted from a source; saved model-facing requests and responses; timestamps and message status; model, routing, privacy, pin, favorite, archive, read, reaction, and latency metadata; and structured answers, charts, and text/table/code/workflow artifacts stored inside a chat. A conversation may carry a project identifier, but project records, project instructions, documents, and the rest of the workspace are not synchronized.

What does not sync: attachment files or attachment records; raw imported documents and their extracted text or OCR; source-citation records; attachment-analysis records; generated-image records or image files; projects and project content; the Document Library and RAG index; memory items; the separate generated-image library; settings; composer drafts; local diagnostics; and raw or temporary voice audio. If source or OCR content is pasted into a message, quoted in a response, or otherwise saved as ordinary chat text, that resulting chat text is synchronized.

Version 3 stores inline chat payloads and sensitive preview/deletion fields using CloudKit encrypted values. Larger encoded chat payloads use assets in the private CloudKit database. Non-content integrity and ordering metadata, including schema version, revision, checksum, and update time, remains ordinary CloudKit record metadata. Apple handles iCloud data under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

iCloud availability, conflicts, and integrity

If no Apple Account is signed into iCloud, or iCloud is restricted or temporarily unavailable, LocalML does not fetch, upload, or confirm pending CloudKit operations. Local chats remain available on the device, the app reports the account status, and sync can be retried after iCloud becomes available. The sync preference may remain enabled while it is waiting.

When the same conversation exists on two devices, LocalML resolves it automatically. The copy with the later conversation update time supplies shared conversation metadata; a tie favors the current device. Messages whose identifiers exist on only one side are retained, while the selected copy is used when the same message identifier has different content. Local-only attachment, citation, image, and analysis fields already present on the current device are preserved. LocalML does not provide a conflict chooser or alternate-version history and is not a real-time collaborative editor.

There is no read-only CloudKit mode. If LocalML encounters an unsupported record version or a record that fails checksum, identity, or deletion-record validation, it rejects the fetched snapshot instead of partially merging it. The existing local workspace remains available and the app reports a sync failure.

iCloud deletion and turning sync off

Turning iCloud chat sync off stops LocalML from scheduling new chat transfers. It does not delete existing records from iCloud and does not clear deletion or retry work already stored on the device. A chat deleted while sync is off is deleted only from that device and may return if sync is enabled again while an older iCloud copy still exists.

When sync is on, LocalML records deletion intent in a protected local journal, uploads a deletion record, and then asks CloudKit to remove the conversation record. A local deletion can finish before CloudKit confirms the remote deletion. Transient failures remain queued for retry; account, permission, or quota problems require user action, and LocalML reports the failure rather than claiming completion. A CloudKit deletion record becomes eligible for cleanup after 365 days when no live conversation record remains, but it may remain longer until a later sync performs that cleanup. Apple may retain iCloud data under its own retention, backup, legal, and account rules.

Permissions

LocalML may request access to photos, camera, microphone, speech recognition, files, or notifications only when a feature needs that permission. You can deny or later change permissions in system settings. Denying a permission may disable the related feature without preventing unrelated app features from working.

Diagnostics and analytics

LocalML 1.0 is not designed to include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Apple or the operating system may provide crash reports, performance diagnostics, App Store analytics, or purchase information according to the user's Apple settings. This information may be used to improve reliability and resolve defects.

Purchases

If LocalML offers purchases or subscriptions, payment processing, receipts, refunds, and purchase restoration are handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit systems. The developer does not receive payment card details.

Support communication

If you contact support, the email address and information you choose to include are used to understand and respond to your request. Do not include private documents, sensitive prompts, health information, passwords, payment information, or other confidential content unless it is strictly necessary.

Deletion and control

You can delete conversations, projects, memories, documents, and artifacts through available in-app controls. You can also revoke system permissions or delete the app. For deletion requests concerning support emails, contact support from the same email address used for the request.

Children

LocalML is not directed to children and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact support if they believe a child submitted personal information through a support message.

Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in LocalML features, Apple platform behavior, data practices, or legal requirements. The date at the top of the page identifies the current revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, use the LocalML Support page.
