LocalML Privacy Policy

LocalML is designed as a privacy-first Apple-native workspace. This policy describes the planned data practices for LocalML 1.0 and will be reviewed before release whenever product behavior changes.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Summary

LocalML is designed to keep prompts, conversations, projects, memories, imported documents, photos, audio, and generated artifacts under the user's control. LocalML 1.0 does not include advertising SDKs, third-party advertising tracking, third-party AI model providers, or CloudKit synchronization.

Content you choose to use

The app may process text you enter and files, documents, photos, screenshots, camera captures, or audio you explicitly select. This content is used only to perform the requested feature, such as answering a question, summarizing a document, reviewing an image, transcribing speech, creating an artifact, or exporting content.

Apple intelligence processing

LocalML is designed around Apple-provided intelligence and system frameworks. Processing is performed on device whenever the selected feature and device support it. If a user explicitly invokes an Apple-provided feature that requires Apple services, the selected content may be handled by Apple under Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

LocalML is not designed to send user content to third-party AI model providers.

Local storage

Chats, projects, project preferences, scoped memory, saved answers, imported-document metadata, generated artifacts, export history, and app settings may be stored locally on the device so the app can provide its features.

LocalML 1.0 does not include CloudKit chat or project synchronization. Removing app content through available in-app controls or deleting the app removes the corresponding local data, subject to normal operating-system backup behavior.

Permissions

LocalML may request access to photos, camera, microphone, speech recognition, files, or notifications only when a feature needs that permission. You can deny or later change permissions in system settings. Denying a permission may disable the related feature without preventing unrelated app features from working.

Diagnostics and analytics

LocalML 1.0 is not designed to include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Apple or the operating system may provide crash reports, performance diagnostics, App Store analytics, or purchase information according to the user's Apple settings. This information may be used to improve reliability and resolve defects.

Purchases

If LocalML offers purchases or subscriptions, payment processing, receipts, refunds, and purchase restoration are handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit systems. The developer does not receive payment card details.

Support communication

If you contact support, the email address and information you choose to include are used to understand and respond to your request. Do not include private documents, sensitive prompts, health information, passwords, payment information, or other confidential content unless it is strictly necessary.

Deletion and control

You can delete conversations, projects, memories, documents, and artifacts through available in-app controls. You can also revoke system permissions or delete the app. For deletion requests concerning support emails, contact support from the same email address used for the request.

Children

LocalML is not directed to children and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact support if they believe a child submitted personal information through a support message.

Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in LocalML features, Apple platform behavior, data practices, or legal requirements. The date at the top of the page identifies the current revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, use the LocalML Support page.
