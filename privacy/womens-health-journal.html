Women's Health Journal Privacy Policy

Women's Health Journal is a private, offline-first iPhone journal for cycle observations, symptoms, medications, appointments, and personal health notes. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no AI, and the developer does not collect data from the app.

Pre-release. Women's Health Journal is not yet available on the App Store. This page describes version 1.0 as prepared for release and will be updated if anything changes before launch.

Summary

Your journal belongs to you. Women's Health Journal stores what you record on your iPhone and, only if you choose, in your own iCloud account. There is no developer-operated server, so the developer has no copy of your journal to sell, share, or disclose. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs and no AI features.

Information you record

Depending on the features you use, the app may store cycle observations, period and flow entries, symptoms, medications, appointments, notes, personal health records, and reports you create. You decide what to record and can edit or delete entries at any time.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional and is requested category by category through Apple's permission screen. The journal works without it. Data read from or written to Apple Health is used only for the features you request. It is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can change access at any time in the Health app.

Storage and iCloud

Entries are stored in the app's container on your iPhone. If your version of the app offers iCloud sync and you turn it on, your journal records are stored in the private database of your Apple Account through Apple's CloudKit so they stay available when you restore or change your iPhone. The developer cannot access your private iCloud data, which Apple holds under the Apple Privacy Policy.

App Lock

If you turn on App Lock, the app asks iOS to confirm your identity with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode before showing your journal. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and is never available to the app or the developer.

Reports and exports

Reports and exports are created on the device only when you ask and are shared through the system share sheet to a destination you choose. A shared file leaves the app's protection, so only send it to people and services you trust.

Reminders and notifications

If you schedule reminders, they are created on the device by iOS. Notification text can appear on the Lock Screen, so choose wording you are comfortable with or turn off notification previews in Settings > Notifications.

Purchases

Women's Health Journal offers a single paid plan processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment card details; Apple handles receipts, refunds, and restoring purchases.

Health and safety

Women's Health Journal is a personal recordkeeping tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment, and does not predict fertile windows or “safe days.” Do not use it for contraception or to plan or avoid pregnancy. For medical concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional; in an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Diagnostics and analytics

Women's Health Journal contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not send usage data to a developer-operated server. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Apple's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer crash reports and performance information. Apple also provides aggregated App Store metrics such as downloads and sales. Apple supplies this information in aggregated or de-identified form, and it is used only to fix problems and improve reliability.

Support communication

If you email support, your email address and whatever you choose to include are used only to answer your request. Messages are handled through the developer's Apple iCloud Mail account and kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and keep a reasonable record of it. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and any personal information that is not needed to understand the problem.

Retention and deletion

Your entries stay on your iPhone until you delete them in the app or delete the app, which removes its local data. Copies in iCloud can be removed through your iCloud storage settings (Settings > [your name] > iCloud). Data written to Apple Health stays in the Health app until you delete it there. The developer holds no copy of your journal. Support emails are deleted on request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and US state privacy laws may give you the right to know what personal data is processed, to access or receive a copy of it, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Women's Health Journal does not send your app data to the developer, so most of it stays on your devices under your direct control. For information you have sent by email, write to azanjani@me.com from the same address so the request can be verified. Requests are answered within 30 days. Personal information is never sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

Women's Health Journal keeps its data in the app's own container, protected by Apple's platform security, including device encryption and Data Protection tied to your device passcode. Optional App Lock adds Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode protection inside the app. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. No storage method is perfectly secure, so only keep information you are comfortable storing on your device.

Children

Women's Health Journal is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. A parent or guardian with a privacy question can contact the developer at any time.

Changes to this policy

This policy is reviewed whenever Women's Health Journal adds a feature that affects personal data, Apple platform behavior changes, or the App Store privacy disclosure changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes are published here before the app version that introduces them.

Contact

The developer and data controller for Women's Health Journal is Ashkan Zanjani. For privacy questions or requests, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, visit Women's Health Journal Support. Apple's own processing is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
