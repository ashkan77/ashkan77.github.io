How to read these pages

One policy, one product, one clear scope.

The installed App Store version and its privacy label remain the final product-specific reference.

01

Start with the app

Select the exact product rather than relying on a generic policy that may describe features the app does not use.

02

Review user controls

Permission, storage, Apple Health, local deletion, and support-message controls are described on the matching page.

03

Check the current release

Features can evolve. The policy date and the App Store privacy label should match the version you use.
