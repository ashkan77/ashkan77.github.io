PrivacySupportCase study

No shared assumptions

Different apps use different features. An app-specific policy should be read together with the privacy label for the installed App Store version.

User control

System permissions can be denied or changed in device settings. Local app records can be removed through available controls or by deleting the app, subject to normal Apple backup and service behavior.

Advertising and tracking

The apps are not designed to sell personal information or use private app content for third-party advertising tracking. Any future change would require updated product behavior, disclosures, and policies.

Support communication

When you email support, your address and the information you choose to include are used to understand and respond to the request. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email azanjani@me.com and identify the app in the subject.
