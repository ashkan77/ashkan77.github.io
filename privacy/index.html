Apple's role

What Apple handles, not the developer.

Some features rely on Apple services. Apple processes that information under the Apple Privacy Policy, and the developer cannot see it.

01

Purchases and refunds

Apple processes paid downloads and in-app purchases. The developer never receives payment card details. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

02

Crash reports and metrics

Apple shares crash reports with developers only if you allow it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. App Store metrics are aggregated by Apple.

03

iCloud and Apple Health

When you turn on iCloud sync or Apple Health, that data lives in your own Apple Account and Health app, where you can review and delete it at any time.

Your rights

Access, correction, and deletion.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK (Law No. 6698), and US state privacy laws give you rights over your personal data. Because the apps keep data on your devices, you can exercise most of them directly in the app or in Settings.

01

On your device

View, export, or delete app data with the controls described in each policy, or delete the app to remove its local data.

02

Information you emailed

To access or delete support messages, email azanjani@me.com from the same address and name the app. Requests are answered within 30 days.

03

Complaints

You can also contact your local data-protection authority, such as the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or your EU or UK supervisory authority.
