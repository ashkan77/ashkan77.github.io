Summary

World Guide is built so that your travel interests, generated guides, camera images, and location stay on your device. The app has no developer-operated server, account system, advertising, or analytics SDK, which matches its App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Earlier versions of this policy described optional name and email collection. World Guide 2.0 does not ask for or collect contact information.

On-device guides and plans

Written guides, audio-tour narration, day plans, and Lens descriptions are generated on your iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence. Your requests and the generated results are saved on the device and are not sent to the developer. Apple Intelligence is provided by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Camera and Lens

Lens uses the camera only while it is open, including when you start it from Visual Intelligence, to recognize a landmark on the device. Images are not uploaded to the developer. You can allow or deny camera access in the system prompt and later in Settings > World Guide.

Location

Location access is optional. If you allow it while using the app, World Guide uses your location on the device to start audio-tour stops as you arrive and to show nearby travel information. Your location is not sent to the developer or used for advertising. You can change access at any time in Settings > World Guide > Location.

Maps and online content

Map imagery and other content that has not been saved yet is downloaded the first time you open it. These requests go to Apple Maps or the relevant content provider, which may receive technical information such as your IP address, device type, and the area requested, under their own terms and privacy practices. Saved content then opens offline.

Siri, widgets, and Live Activities

Siri requests, the City of the Day and Passport widgets, and audio-tour Live Activities on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island are handled by Apple system features according to your Apple settings. World Guide supplies only the travel content needed to display them.

Saved content on your device

Saved guides, offline map imagery, day plans, Passport stamps, favorite places, phrasebooks, reading progress, language, and preferences are stored on the device so the app works offline and remembers your choices.

Purchases

World Guide is free to download. World Guide Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment card details; Apple handles receipts, refunds, Restore Purchases, and Family Sharing according to your Apple Account and family settings.

Diagnostics and analytics

World Guide contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not send usage data to a developer-operated server. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Apple's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer crash reports and performance information. Apple also provides aggregated App Store metrics such as downloads and sales. Apple supplies this information in aggregated or de-identified form, and it is used only to fix problems and improve reliability.

Support communication

If you email support, your email address and whatever you choose to include are used only to answer your request. Messages are handled through the developer's Apple iCloud Mail account and kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and keep a reasonable record of it. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and any personal information that is not needed to understand the problem.

Retention and deletion

Information saved by World Guide stays on your device until you remove it in the app or delete the app, which removes its local data. The developer holds no copy of your travel content, so there is nothing to delete on a developer server. Support emails are deleted on request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and US state privacy laws may give you the right to know what personal data is processed, to access or receive a copy of it, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

World Guide does not send your app data to the developer, so most of it stays on your devices under your direct control. For information you have sent by email, write to azanjani@me.com from the same address so the request can be verified. Requests are answered within 30 days. Personal information is never sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

World Guide keeps its data in the app's own container, protected by Apple's platform security, including device encryption and Data Protection tied to your device passcode. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. No storage method is perfectly secure, so only keep information you are comfortable storing on your device.

Children

World Guide is a general travel app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. A parent or guardian with a privacy question can contact the developer at any time.

Changes to this policy

This policy is reviewed whenever World Guide adds a feature that affects personal data, Apple platform behavior changes, or the App Store privacy disclosure changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes are published here before the app version that introduces them.

Contact

The developer and data controller for World Guide is Ashkan Zanjani. For privacy questions or requests, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, visit World Guide Support. Apple's own processing is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
