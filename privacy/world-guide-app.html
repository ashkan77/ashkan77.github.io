World Guide App Privacy Policy

World Guide App provides destination content, maps, weather information, favorites, and travel-oriented browsing. This policy describes the data used to provide those features.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Contact information

World Guide App's current App Store privacy disclosure identifies name and email address as contact information that may be collected and linked to the user for app functionality. This information is used only when a user chooses to provide it for a feature or support request.

How contact information is used

Name and email address may be used to provide the requested app functionality, communicate about a request, prevent misuse, or respond to support. Contact information is not sold and is not used for third-party advertising tracking.

Location and maps

The app may request optional location access to show nearby or location-relevant travel information. You control location permission through the system prompt and device settings. The developer does not use precise location for advertising tracking.

Online content

Maps, weather, images, and other online destination content may require network requests to Apple or content providers. Those services may process technical information such as IP address, device information, request details, or approximate location under their own terms and privacy practices.

Favorites and app preferences

Favorite cities, attractions, reading state, and preferences may be stored locally on the device to provide the experience you choose. Local app data can be removed through available app controls or by deleting the app.

Diagnostics

Apple or the operating system may provide crash reports, performance diagnostics, and App Store analytics according to the user's Apple settings. The app is not designed to use personal travel data for third-party advertising tracking.

Support communication

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to respond to the request. Support messages may be retained as needed to resolve the issue and maintain a record of the communication.

Deletion and requests

You can delete local favorites and app data through available controls or by deleting the app. To request access to or deletion of name, email, or support information you submitted, contact support from the same email address so the request can be verified.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact support if they believe a child submitted contact information.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers, or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email azanjani@me.com. For troubleshooting, open World Guide App Support.
