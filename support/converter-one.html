Unexpected result

Check the source and target units, the decimal separator, and the value you entered, then clear the input and try again. For temperature, fuel economy, and other non-linear categories, make sure the conversion direction is correct.

Categories and units

Converter One covers many categories, including length, weight, temperature, speed, energy, data, and torque. If a unit you need is missing, email its name and the category it belongs to.

Search and favorites

Check the spelling, clear filters, and reopen the category. If favorites do not update, close and reopen the app. Resetting or reinstalling the app removes locally stored favorites and settings.

Number formatting

Number formatting follows your device language and region. If a result appears in an unexpected format, check the region, decimal separator, and preferred measurement system in Settings.

Offline use

All core conversions work offline. An internet connection is not needed for normal calculations.

Mac and Apple Vision Pro

On a Mac with Apple silicon, Converter One runs its iPad version, so the layout follows the iPad design. On Apple Vision Pro, look at a control and pinch to select it; the 3D view responds to the same gestures.

Purchases and Family Sharing

Converter One is a paid app that supports Family Sharing. To reinstall, sign in with the Apple Account that bought it or a member of the same family group. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

The developer does not collect conversion values, search history, or personal information. Read the Converter One Privacy Policy for details.

What to include

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, source unit, target unit, entered value, displayed result, and the result you expected. Remove personal details from screenshots before sending them.

Contact

Email azanjani@me.com with the app name in the subject. Support is available in English and Turkish (Türkçe destek verilir).
