Running a session

Remove the case, disconnect cables and accessories, and hold the iPhone with the speaker openings facing down. Choose a session length and start; you can pause or stop at any time. Run the speaker test before and after to compare the sound. If the app suggests it, tap or tilt the phone gently. Never shake it hard.

No sound is playing

Raise the media volume and make sure audio is not routed to AirPods, a Bluetooth speaker, CarPlay, or another external device. Stop the current session, reopen DropClear, and try again.

Sound is quiet or distorted

Remove cases or accessories that cover the speaker openings and keep the speaker area clear. Do not press the phone against fabric or a flat surface. Distortion that persists can mean moisture or hardware damage that an app cannot repair.

Speaker test

The speaker test plays a short reference sound so you can hear whether the output sounds clearer. It only plays audio. It does not record with the microphone or measure the speaker electronically.

Haptics are not noticeable

Haptic strength and availability vary by device, system state, accessibility settings, and the active session. The sound part of the session still runs when haptic feedback is reduced or unavailable.

When to stop

Stop the session if the iPhone becomes unusually warm, makes abnormal mechanical sounds, shows a liquid-detection or charging warning, or looks physically damaged. Do not charge a wet device, insert objects into the speaker, or use compressed air or heat.

Persistent audio problems

DropClear cannot diagnose internal damage or guarantee that liquid is removed. If the sound is still muffled after the iPhone has had time to dry, or the phone had significant liquid exposure, follow Apple Support guidance and contact Apple or an authorized service provider.

DropClear Pro

DropClear Pro is an optional in-app purchase. If Pro is missing after reinstalling or on another iPhone, use the app's restore purchases option while signed in to the same Apple Account. Payments, purchase history, and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

DropClear does not use the microphone, needs no account, and does not collect data for the developer. Read the DropClear: Water Eject Privacy Policy for details.

What to include

Include the DropClear version, iPhone model, iOS version, the session length you chose, whether Bluetooth or another audio route was connected, whether sound or haptics were missing, and the exact steps that produced the problem. Remove personal details from screenshots before sending them.

Contact
