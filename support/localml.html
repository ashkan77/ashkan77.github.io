Before contacting support

Restart LocalML, confirm the device has enough free storage, install the latest available operating-system update, and try the action again in a new conversation. For document or image issues, test with a smaller file to identify whether the problem is file-specific.

Intelligence unavailable

Some LocalML features depend on Apple intelligence capabilities, supported hardware, installed system resources, language availability, and regional availability. Check the app's Intelligence settings and the related Apple system settings on the device.

Documents and visual context

Confirm that Files, Photos, or Camera permission is available for the feature you are using. Password-protected, damaged, unusually large, or unsupported files may not import or analyze correctly.

Voice

Confirm microphone and speech-recognition permissions, speak after the recording indicator appears, and review the transcript before sending. Recognition quality can vary by language, background noise, device support, and downloaded system resources.

Optional iCloud chat sync

iCloud chat sync is optional and off by default. Enable it in Settings > Data & Privacy. It uses the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account and is not required for the local workspace. It synchronizes supported conversation content, not the full LocalML workspace. Attachment files, attachment analysis, source-citation records, generated-image records and files, projects, documents, RAG data, memory, raw voice audio, settings, drafts, and diagnostics are not synchronized. Text pasted or quoted into a normal chat message, including a sent voice transcript, is chat text and does synchronize.

iCloud sync is unavailable

Confirm that the device is signed into an Apple Account for iCloud, iCloud is not restricted, and chat sync is enabled in LocalML. If the status says Sign in to iCloud, Restricted, or Temporarily unavailable, your local chats remain usable and LocalML does not fetch, upload, or confirm pending CloudKit work. The sync switch may remain on while waiting; sign in or restore iCloud access, then choose Try Sync Again or Sync Now.

A deleted chat returned

Turning sync off does not remove existing iCloud records. If you delete a chat while sync is off, that deletion applies only to the current device; an older iCloud copy can return when sync is enabled again. To delete a synchronized chat from iCloud, keep sync on, delete the chat, allow the Undo period to finish, and wait for Deleted from iCloud. If LocalML reports a failure, restore iCloud access and retry. Local deletion can finish before CloudKit confirms remote deletion.

Conflicts and record-integrity errors

LocalML automatically merges conversations; it does not provide a conflict chooser or alternate-version history. For the same conversation, the later conversation update supplies shared metadata, messages present on only one device are retained, and the selected copy is used if the same message identifier differs. If a CloudKit record has an unsupported version or fails checksum, identity, or deletion-record validation, LocalML rejects that fetched snapshot instead of partially merging it. Your existing local workspace remains available. Retry once; if the error continues, contact support with the exact status message but do not send private chat content.

Storage and deletion

Local workspace categories can be reviewed and deleted in Settings > Data & Privacy. Turning iCloud chat sync off stops new sync activity but does not delete records already stored in iCloud or clear pending retry/deletion work. When sync is on, LocalML queues a protected deletion record and asks CloudKit to remove the conversation; transient failures retry and account, permission, or quota errors require attention. See the LocalML Privacy Policy for the exact synchronized fields and retention behavior.

Privacy

Do not send private documents, confidential prompts, passwords, payment information, or sensitive screenshots in a support email. Review the LocalML Privacy Policy for data-handling details.

What to include

Include the LocalML version, device model, operating-system version, selected intelligence mode, the exact steps that produced the issue, and the text of any visible error. A redacted screenshot is helpful when it contains no sensitive content.
