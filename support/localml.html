Before contacting support

Restart LocalML, make sure the device has enough free storage, install the latest operating-system update, and try the action again in a new conversation.

Apple Intelligence is unavailable

Built-in AI features need an Apple Intelligence–capable device with Apple Intelligence turned on, a supported device and app language and region, and a ready Apple Foundation model. LocalML intentionally does not send the request to a third-party AI provider when those requirements are not met.

Imported model files

If your version supports importing a model, choose a compatible model file through the Files app and make sure the device has enough free storage and memory for it. Large models can load slowly or fail on devices with less memory; try a smaller model if loading stops.

Documents and visual context

Confirm that Files, Photos, or Camera access is available for the feature you are using. Password-protected, damaged, very large, or unsupported files may not import or analyze correctly. Selected images and extracted text are processed on the device.

Voice input is unavailable

Check LocalML's Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions in Settings, speak after the recording indicator appears, and review the transcript before sending. LocalML requires an on-device Apple Speech path and has no third-party transcription fallback. Spoken replies use installed Apple system voices.

iCloud chat sync is unavailable

iCloud chat sync is optional and off by default. Confirm you are signed in to iCloud and have turned on chat sync in Settings > Data & Privacy. If the status reports an account, restriction, network, quota, or temporary-availability problem, fix it and choose Try Sync Again or Sync Now.

Web Source Access is unavailable

Web Source Access is off by default. Turn it on in Settings > Data & Privacy to read a public HTTPS address you provide or a page found through General Web Research. LocalML blocks local, private, reserved, credential-bearing, and otherwise unsafe addresses.

General Web Research or Brave Search is unavailable

General Web Research is off by default and needs Web Source Access plus a Brave Search API key saved in the Keychain. Confirm both settings, check that the current mode or project allows online sources, and check your Brave Search API quota.

Apple Online Research is unavailable

Apple Online Research uses Apple Maps for place, address, landmark, and point-of-interest requests. Confirm it is on in Settings > Data & Privacy and the device is online. LocalML does not use your precise location, so name a city or neighborhood instead of “near me.”

Share to LocalML is not visible

In Safari, Files, or Photos, open the Share Sheet and use Edit Actions to add LocalML. The extension accepts links, selected text, images, and supported files. Shared content stays editable, and nothing is sent until you review it and tap Send.

Storage and deletion

Review and delete local workspace categories in Settings > Data & Privacy. Turning iCloud chat sync off stops new sync activity but does not delete records already in iCloud. While sync is on, LocalML queues deletions and reports failures rather than claiming remote deletion before CloudKit confirms it.

Reporting an AI response

To report an incorrect, biased, offensive, sexual, or otherwise concerning response, choose Report this response. You can review the report before sharing it; nothing is sent automatically.

Purchases

If LocalML offers an optional paid upgrade, Apple processes the purchase. If it is missing after reinstalling or on another device, use the app's restore purchases option while signed in to the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

LocalML keeps your workspace on the device and uses no third-party AI providers. Read the LocalML Privacy Policy for details.

What to include

Include the LocalML version and build, device model, operating-system version, the feature involved, and the exact error shown. Do not send private documents, passwords, health or financial information, or a full chat export. Remove personal details from screenshots before sending them.

Contact

Email azanjani@me.com with the app name in the subject. Support is available in English and Turkish (Türkçe destek verilir).
