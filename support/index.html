Common questions

Quick answers for every app.

Purchases, refunds, and Apple Account issues are handled by Apple; the answers below point you to the right place.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes every App Store payment, so refunds are requested from Apple, not the developer. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple Account you used, and choose the purchase.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account. Paid apps (Watch Audio Player, Converter One, BMI Checker Plus) can be downloaded again from your purchase history in the App Store. For in-app purchases such as World Guide Pro and DropClear Pro, open the app and use its restore purchases option.

Does Family Sharing work?

Family Sharing is managed by Apple and shown on each app's App Store page. When it is available, family members can download the app or unlock the purchase with their own Apple Account in the same family group.

How do I find the app version?

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings > General > iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) and select the app; the version is shown at the top. Many apps also show it in their own Settings or About screen.

How do I delete my data?

App data is stored on your device, so you can remove it with the app's delete controls or by deleting the app. Data you chose to keep in iCloud or Apple Health is managed in Settings > [your name] > iCloud and in the Health app. The developer holds no copy of your app data.

To delete a support conversation, email azanjani@me.com from the address you used.

Why is a feature unavailable on my device?

Features built on Apple Intelligence, such as World Guide's guides and LocalML's built-in AI, need a supported device, a supported language and region, and Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Each app's support page lists its exact requirements.

I found a security or privacy problem.

Email azanjani@me.com with “Security Report” and the app name in the subject. Describe the steps to reproduce it, and do not include other people's personal data.

Can I get support in Turkish?

Yes. You can write in English or Turkish. Evet, Türkçe yazabilirsiniz.

Before you send

A useful report is specific and safe.

These three checks reduce follow-up questions and protect your information.

01

Describe the exact path

Write the steps in order, what appeared, what you expected, and whether the issue happens every time.

02

Include the environment

Add the app version, device model, operating-system version, permission state, and any relevant file or unit details.

03

Remove sensitive content

Do not send passwords, payment card details, private documents, personal audio, or health information.
