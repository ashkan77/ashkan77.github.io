Before you send

A useful report is specific and safe.

These three checks reduce follow-up questions and help protect your information.

01

Describe the exact path

Write the steps in order, what appeared, what you expected, and whether the issue happens every time.

02

Include the environment

Add the app version, device model, operating-system version, permission state, and relevant file or unit details.

03

Remove sensitive content

Do not send passwords, payment card information, private documents, personal audio, or unnecessary health information.

Direct support

Still need help after checking the app page?

Use the app name in the email subject and include a concise, reproducible description. App Store payments, refunds, and purchase history are managed by Apple.
