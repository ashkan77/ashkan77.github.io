What to include

For faster support, include the app name, app version, device model, operating system version, the steps that caused the issue, and what you expected to happen.

Protect sensitive information

Do not email passwords, payment information, private health details, private documents, or other sensitive content. Remove personal information from screenshots whenever possible.

Purchases

App Store payments, refunds, family sharing, and purchase history are managed by Apple. For an app-access issue, include the app name and a description, but do not send payment card details.
