Importing audio

Import from the Files app or share a file to Watch Audio Player from another app's Share Sheet. Supported formats are AAC, AIFF, CAF, FLAC, M4A, M4B, MP3, and WAV. Copy-protected files and streaming-service downloads cannot be imported.

Audio will not transfer

Keep the iPhone and Apple Watch close together with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on, open the app on both devices, and send the file again. Large files and long queues take longer and need free storage on the watch. A transfer is marked complete only after the file passes an integrity check on the watch.

Audio will not play

Confirm the file finished transferring and is not damaged, the watch volume is audible, and the Apple Watch speaker or Bluetooth headphones are available. Pair headphones in Settings > Bluetooth on the watch. If only one item fails, remove it and import it again.

Controls and volume

Use the Now Playing controls on the watch and turn the Digital Crown to change the volume.

Watch library

Browse, search, favorite, rename, and remove audio directly on Apple Watch. If the library looks out of date, open the app on both devices so they can refresh.

Storage

Delete audio you no longer need and check free storage on the watch in Settings > General > Storage. Deleting the app removes its stored audio, so keep original copies of important files.

Offline playback

Audio that has finished transferring plays on Apple Watch without an internet connection or your iPhone nearby. Files that are still transferring or exist only on the iPhone are not available offline on the watch.

Widgets

iPhone and Apple Watch widgets show transfer and library status. If a widget looks out of date, open the app, then remove and add the widget again if needed.

Purchases and Family Sharing

Purchases, purchase history, and Family Sharing are managed by Apple. To reinstall, sign in with the Apple Account that bought the app. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

The developer does not collect your audio, file names, or listening data. Read the Watch Audio Player Privacy Policy for details.

What to include

Include the app version, iPhone and Apple Watch models, iOS and watchOS versions, the file format and approximate size, and the exact step where the issue occurs. Do not email audio files unless asked. Remove personal details from screenshots before sending them.
