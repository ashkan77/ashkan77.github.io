Health and safety

Women's Health Journal is not a medical device. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatment, or predict fertile windows or “safe days,” and it must not be used for contraception or to plan or avoid pregnancy. For medical concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional; in an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Getting started

The journal works offline and without an account. Start by adding an entry. Apple Health access and App Lock are optional and can be turned on later.

Apple Health permission

If data from Apple Health is missing, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, choose Apps under Privacy, select Women's Health Journal, and turn on the categories you want to share. The journal itself does not need Health access.

App Lock

App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode through iOS. If unlocking fails, make sure Face ID or Touch ID is set up and allowed for the app in Settings > Face ID & Passcode (or Touch ID & Passcode).

iCloud

If iCloud sync is available and turned on, make sure you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, iCloud is allowed for the app, and you have iCloud storage available. Sync timing is controlled by Apple and may not be immediate.

Reports and exports

Choose the date range and records, then share through the system share sheet. Check the file before you send it, because it can contain sensitive health information.

Changing the language

The app is available in English and Turkish and follows your iPhone language. To use a different language for the app only, open Settings > Apps > Women's Health Journal > Language.

Purchases

Women's Health Journal has one paid plan. If it does not appear after reinstalling or on a new iPhone, use the app's restore purchases option while signed in to the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data and deletion

Delete individual entries in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from that iPhone. Copies in iCloud and Apple Health are managed in your iCloud settings and the Health app.

Privacy

The developer does not collect journal entries, health data, or usage data. Read the Women's Health Journal Privacy Policy for details.

What to include

Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, the affected feature, whether Apple Health, App Lock, or iCloud is turned on, and the exact steps that caused the issue. Never send journal entries, cycle details, or health records. Remove personal details from screenshots before sending them.

Contact
