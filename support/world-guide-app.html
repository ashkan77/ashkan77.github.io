Guides, tours, or Lens not available

Written guides, audio tours, day plans, and Lens need an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri and a supported device and Siri language. After you turn Apple Intelligence on, its models download in the background; keep the device on Wi-Fi and power until it is ready.

World Guide also installs on Mac computers with Apple silicon and on Apple Vision Pro, but features that depend on Apple Intelligence may not be available there.

Using World Guide offline

Open a guide, plan, or tour once while preparing your trip so it is saved on the device together with its map imagery. Saved content stays available in airplane mode. New destinations and fresh map imagery need a connection the first time.

Audio tours and Live Activities

Tours play each stop as you arrive, so allow location access while using the app and keep Live Activities turned on for World Guide in Settings to follow the tour on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Lens and Visual Intelligence

Allow camera access when asked, or turn it on in Settings > World Guide > Camera. Point the camera at the landmark in good light and hold steady. Recognition runs on the device and may not identify every landmark.

World Guide Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and unlocks audio tours, unlimited plans, Lens, and phrasebooks. If Pro does not appear on another device or for a family member, use Restore Purchases in the app while signed in to the same Apple Account or Family Sharing group. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Siri and widgets

Try “Tell me about Hagia Sophia with World Guide.” If Siri does not respond, open the app once and check that Siri is allowed for World Guide. Add the City of the Day or Passport widget from the Home Screen editor.

Maps not loading

Map imagery that has not been saved yet needs an internet connection. Turn off any network restriction that may block the request and reopen the destination. Online services can occasionally be unavailable or slow.

Location access

Location is optional and controlled in Settings > World Guide > Location. If a location-based feature is not working, allow access while using the app and confirm that Location Services are on.

Saved guides and favorites

Make sure the save or favorite action finished, then reopen Saved. Deleting the app removes saved guides, offline map imagery, plans, Passport stamps, and favorites from the device.

Content accuracy

Guides are generated by Apple Intelligence on your device and can contain mistakes. Opening hours, prices, transport, weather, and entry rules change, so confirm time-sensitive details with the official venue, transport provider, or local authority before you travel.

Changing the app language

World Guide follows your device language. To use a different language for the app only, open Settings > Apps > World Guide > Language.

Privacy

World Guide does not collect data for the developer: guides, camera images, and location stay on your device. Read the World Guide Privacy Policy for details.

What to include

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, app language, whether Apple Intelligence is on, the destination or feature involved, your connection type, permission status, and the exact steps that caused the issue. Remove personal details from screenshots before sending them.
