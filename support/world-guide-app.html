World Guide Support

Help with on-device guides, audio tours, trip plans, Lens, Siri, widgets, offline use, World Guide Pro, and travel content.

Email supportPrivacy Policy

Guides, tours, or Lens not available

Written guides, audio tours, plans, and Lens require an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, running iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. The first generation of a guide can take a moment while on-device models are ready; saved guides then open offline.

Using World Guide offline

Open a guide, plan, or tour once while preparing your trip so it is saved on the device, together with its map imagery. Saved content stays available in airplane mode. New destinations and fresh map imagery need a connection the first time.

Audio tours and Live Activities

Tours play each stop as you arrive, so allow location access while using the app and keep Live Activities enabled for World Guide in Settings to see the tour on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

World Guide Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and unlocks audio tours, unlimited plans, Lens, and phrasebooks. If Pro does not appear on another device or for a family member, use Restore Purchases in the app while signed in to the same Apple Account or Family Sharing group. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Siri and widgets

Try “Tell me about Hagia Sophia with World Guide.” If Siri does not respond, open the app once and check that Siri is enabled for World Guide. Add the City of the Day or Travel Passport widget from the Home Screen editor.

Maps not loading

Map imagery that has not been saved yet needs an internet connection. Disable any network restriction that may block the request and reopen the destination. Online services can occasionally be unavailable or delayed.

Location access

Location is optional and controlled through system settings. If a location-based feature is not working, confirm World Guide has the appropriate location permission and that Location Services are enabled.

Images or content

Reopen the page on a stable connection. If one destination consistently shows missing or incorrect content, include the country, city, attraction, and a screenshot when contacting support.

Favorites

Make sure the favorite action completed and reopen the Favorites view. Reinstalling the app may remove locally stored favorites, so use caution before deleting the app.

Content accuracy

Travel information, weather, opening details, and third-party destination information can change. Confirm time-sensitive details with the official venue, transportation provider, government source, or local authority before traveling.

Privacy

Review the World Guide Privacy Policy for on-device guides, camera, location, purchases, saved content, and deletion requests.

What to include

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, app language, whether Apple Intelligence is on, the affected destination or feature, connection type, permission status, and the exact steps that caused the issue.
