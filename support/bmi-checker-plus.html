Unexpected BMI result

BMI is weight divided by height squared, so check the measurement system, height, weight, and decimal separator first; a unit mix-up is the most common cause. Standard adult BMI categories do not apply to children, teens, or pregnancy, and they can misclassify very muscular people.

Apple Health permission

In the Health app, tap your profile picture, choose Apps under Privacy, select BMI Checker Plus, and turn on the categories the feature needs. Then reopen the app.

Apple Watch

Install the watch app from the Watch app on iPhone (My Watch > Available Apps) or from the App Store on Apple Watch. Results saved on the watch can be written to Apple Health when you allow it.

History and iCloud sync

Make sure each device is signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and that iCloud is allowed for BMI Checker Plus in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Sync timing is controlled by Apple and may not be immediate.

Hydration, reminders, and widgets

Confirm notification permission is on and the reminder schedule is active. If a widget is not refreshing, open the app to update its data, then remove and add the widget again if needed.

PDF and CSV exports

Make sure there is history to export and choose a destination in the share sheet. Exported files can contain health information, so store and share them carefully.

Health and safety

BMI Checker Plus is a general wellness utility, not a medical device. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency advice. Contact a qualified healthcare professional with medical questions.

Purchases and Family Sharing

BMI Checker Plus is a paid app that supports Family Sharing. To reinstall, sign in with the Apple Account that bought it or a member of the same family group. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

The developer does not collect BMI, hydration, Apple Health, or history data. Read the BMI Checker Plus Privacy Policy for details.

What to include

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, unit system, the affected feature, and the exact steps that caused the issue. Do not send Health records or medical information. Remove personal details from screenshots before sending them.
