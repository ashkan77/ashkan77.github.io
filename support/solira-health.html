Health and safety

Solira Health is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medication instructions, or emergency medical advice. For medical concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.

Apple Health permission

Use the Health app or system settings to review the Health categories available to Solira Health. If a summary or signal is missing, confirm the relevant Health permission and that the source data exists in Apple Health.

Reminders and widgets

Confirm notification permission is enabled and the reminder schedule is active. For a widget that is not refreshing, open the app to update its data and remove and re-add the widget if needed.

Ask Solira and voice

Generated responses should be reviewed before relying on them or saving anything. For voice issues, confirm microphone and speech-recognition permissions and review the transcript before sending.

Data and deletion

Current product direction keeps user records locally and does not include CloudKit synchronization. Use available in-app delete controls, manage Apple Health data in the Health app, revoke permissions in system settings, or delete the app.

Privacy

Do not send private medical records, passwords, payment information, or unnecessary sensitive health details in a support email. Review the Solira Health Privacy Policy.

What to include

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, affected feature, permission status, and exact steps that caused the issue. Redact personal health details from screenshots whenever possible.
