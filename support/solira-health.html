Health and safety

Solira Health is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medication instructions, or emergency medical advice. For medical concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.

Apple Health permission

In the Health app, tap your profile picture, choose Apps under Privacy, and review the categories available to Solira Health. If a summary or signal is missing, confirm the permission and that the source data exists in Apple Health.

Reminders and widgets

Confirm notification permission is on and the reminder schedule is active. If a widget is not refreshing, open the app to update its data, then remove and add the widget again if needed.

Ask Solira and voice

Review generated responses before relying on them or saving anything. For voice issues, check microphone and speech-recognition permissions and review the transcript before sending.

Data and deletion

Records are kept locally and are not synchronized with CloudKit. Use in-app delete controls, manage Apple Health data in the Health app, revoke permissions in Settings, or delete the app.

Privacy

Health records stay on your iPhone, and the developer does not collect health data. Read the Solira Health Privacy Policy for details.

What to include

Include the app build, device model, operating-system version, affected feature, permission status, and exact steps. Do not send medical records or health details. Remove personal details from screenshots before sending them.
